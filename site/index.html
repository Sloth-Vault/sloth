A key asks to move everything.

Maybe it's you. Maybe it's someone who got your seed phrase through a fake mint page. The vault can't tell, so it treats both the same way: it writes the request on-chain and moves nothing.

One key is never enough.

The vault has three keys: your main wallet, a backup on another device, and a guardian you choose. Any move needs two of them. A thief with one key has a request nobody will sign.

The second key says yes. The clock starts.

Both keys see the same thing, read from the chain: the amount, and the address it goes to. If the site can't read a request as a plain transfer, it tells you not to approve.

12:00

Now nothing can hurry it.

The wait lives in the Squads program, so no site, team or signer can skip it. Two keys together can cancel at any point before release. Pick 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days when you create the vault.

23:59

Lost a key? Swap it.

The other two keys replace it. The swap waits out the same clock, and it voids every request still waiting for votes, so a thief's open request dies with the old key.

24:00

Released.

Main or backup sends the final transaction and the funds arrive at the address both keys agreed on. For money you spend every day, use a normal wallet. SLOTH is for the part you can't afford to lose.

What it doesn't do.

Two stolen keys beat it.

Two keys can approve a move. After that, your remaining key can't cancel alone, because cancelling also takes two. Keep the three keys on separate devices, and pick a guardian you trust.

Your guardian holds real power.

A guardian can't start a move, but it can be the second vote on a thief's request. It can also refuse to vote when you need it.

It isn't private.

The vault and everything it does are public on Solana, like any wallet.

It's slow on purpose.

There's no emergency exit and no fast lane. If you need the money today, it's the wrong place for it.

You're trusting Squads.

Funds sit in a Squads v4 multisig. SLOTH holds no key and runs no contract of its own, and it isn't an audited product. Start small.

This site can go away.

Your vault won't. Any Squads v4 tool can open it with your keys.
